# Janun Implementation Plan

> Execute inline with executing-plans. The user provided the build spec and phases and instructed development to start without routine approval gates.

**Goal:** A runnable multilingual citizen-service directory, source-review workflow, evidence-based assistant and journey planner.
**Architecture:** Next.js frontend consumes FastAPI. PostgreSQL holds versioned service knowledge and pgvector embeddings.
**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Next.js, Tailwind, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL, pgvector, Alembic.
**Spec:** ../../architecture.md and the supplied master prompt.

## Global Constraints
- No fabricated government facts or test fixtures in production.
- Human review before authoritative publication.
- No secrets or personal identifiers in frontend or logs.
- Honest unavailable-provider, missing-evidence and stale-source states.

## Review Focus
- Unpublished or stale knowledge must not leak via search, service details or citations.
- Admin mutation without authorized same-origin session must fail.
- Ingestion of private addresses or oversized documents must fail.
- Journey coverage gaps must not become invented requirements.
- Failed providers and empty databases must produce usable error/empty states.
